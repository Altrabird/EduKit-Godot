# Juara Kuiz Sekolah

**Main sekarang / Play now: https://altrabird.github.io/Juara-Kuiz-Sekolah/**

A TV-style quiz show for Malaysian primary schools (KSSR, Tahun 1–6), in Bahasa Melayu
and English, hosted by Robo the tutor robot and Kenny the hornbill. Runs in any modern
browser on school PCs, Chromebooks and tablets. There's nothing to install.

## How to play

1. **Main** (solo) or **Mod Kelas** (2–4 teams taking turns on one projected screen).
2. Pick a **Tahun** and one of 11 KSSR subjects, or **Campuran** for a mix of subjects.
   Sejarah and RBT start from Tahun 4, as in KSSR.
3. Clear 5 stages: Kelas → Sekolah → Daerah → Negeri → Kebangsaan. Each stage has 10
   questions, and the timer gets shorter at each stage. The 10th question is the
   **boss question**, worth double points.
4. **Combo**: 3 right answers in a row = ×2 points, 5 in a row = ×3.
5. **Lifelines**, once per round (per team in class mode): **50:50**, **Petunjuk**
   (a hint from Robo) and **Langkau** (skip the question).
6. Earn 1–3 stars per stage. One star opens the next stage.

Questions come from 193 hand-written KSSR questions with recorded voice, plus maths
generated for each Tahun and picture questions ("Apakah ini?") drawn from 323 subject
illustrations, so a round is rarely the same twice. Tap the speaker to hear a question
read aloud in a Malaysian Malay voice, so younger pupils can listen instead of read.

## For teachers

Hold the **Guru** button for 1.5 seconds to open the question editor. Add your own
questions (BM, with optional English and a picture). They're saved in this browser and
appear in every round. Use **Muat turun** (download) to share a set as a file, and
**Import** to paste a set from a colleague.
