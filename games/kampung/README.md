# Kampungku Sihat

**Main sekarang / Play now: https://altrabird.github.io/Kampungku-Sihat/**

The village has lost its colour. Every good habit brings it back. A village map for
Tahun 1–3 with six activities, and the map regains its colour as pupils earn stars.

| Activity | What you do | KSSR |
|---|---|---|
| **Pinggan Sihat** | Drag food onto the Malaysian Healthy Plate: ½ vegetables & fruit, ¼ grains, ¼ protein. Burgers and fizzy drinks go to "Kurangkan". | PJPK |
| **Kitar Semula** | Sort items into Malaysia's recycling bins: blue = paper, brown = glass, orange = plastic & cans, plus the food-waste bin. | Pendidikan Moral |
| **Basuh Tangan** | Tap the six hand-washing steps in order, and keep scrubbing the soap for a full 20 seconds (the timer pauses if you stop). | PJPK |
| **Bantu Jiran** | Six everyday choices: a found wallet, a heavy shopping bag, a friend who falls, owning up, a new pupil, saving electricity. | Pendidikan Moral |
| **Sudut Ibadah** | Put the rukun of wudhu in order, then pick the things that belong to Hari Raya Aidilfitri. Objects only, no figures. | Pendidikan Islam |
| **Kompang & Warna** | Copy kompang rhythms (DUM = centre, pak = edge), then mix two primary colours to make green, orange and purple. | Pendidikan Muzik · Seni Visual |

In Bahasa Melayu or English (BM / EN). A pupil guide speaks every instruction in a
Malaysian Malay voice.
