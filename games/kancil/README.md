# Kancil & Suku Kata

**Main sekarang / Play now: https://altrabird.github.io/Kancil-Suku-Kata/**

A reading platformer for Tahun 1–2. Sang Kancil wants to cross the river and the forest,
but Sang Buaya and Sang Harimau are in the way, and every gate opens only with the
right word.

- **Run and jump** through the kampung and the rainforest, and collect the syllable
  tiles (*bo*, *la*…). Every tile says its syllable aloud in a Malaysian Malay voice.
- **Hop across crocodiles' backs** to cross the river, as in the folk tale. Jump over
  the tigers and the pencil spikes.
- **Spell the word at each gate**: drag the syllables into order under the picture
  (*bo* + *la* = **bola**). The word is spoken, and the gate opens.
- 11 BM levels with 22 picture words, starting with 2-syllable words (bola, roti,
  susu…) and moving to 3-syllable words (kereta, buaya, perahu…). Every word uses only
  open syllables (KV), as taught in Tahun 1.
- **English mode**: 10 levels that spell 20 picture words letter by letter (cat, dog,
  fish, frog…).
- Stars depend on how few wrong drops and bumps you make. There are no lives and no
  game over: falling just returns you to the last gate.

Controls: arrow keys / A–D to move, Space / Up to jump. On tablets and phones,
on-screen buttons appear.

## Source

Built with Godot 4.7 on the EduKit asset kit:
https://github.com/Altrabird/EduKit-Godot (game source in `games/kancil/`).
This repository holds the web build only.

Fonts: Fredoka and Andika (SIL OFL). Voices: Microsoft neural TTS (ms-MY Yasmin,
en-SG Luna) via edge-tts.
