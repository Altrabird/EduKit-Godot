# Kedai Runcit Pak Ali

**Main sekarang / Play now: https://altrabird.github.io/Kedai-Runcit-Pak-Ali/**

Run Pak Ali's village shop and practise KSSR Matematik (money, change, time and
fractions), in Bahasa Melayu or English.

A shop day goes like this:
1. **Open the shop on time.** Set the clock to the opening time. Tahun 1 uses whole
   hours with buttons; Tahun 2 drags to half hours; Tahun 3–4 drag in 5-minute steps.
2. **Serve the customers.** Each pupil shows a picture shopping list. Tap the right
   items on the shelf.
3. **Work out the total.** Pick the correct total from four choices. In Tahun 4 some
   customers bring a discount coupon (10%, 20% or 50%).
4. **Give exact change.** The customer pays with a note; tap coins and notes from the
   drawer until the change is exact.
5. **Cut fractions** (Tahun 2+). One customer a day wants ½, ¼, ¾, ⅓, ⅝ … of a
   watermelon. Tap the slices of the fraction pie.

Prices follow the Tahun: Tahun 1 uses whole ringgit up to RM10, Tahun 2 steps of 50 sen,
Tahun 3 steps of 10 sen, and Tahun 4 steps of 5 sen with discounts. Ten days per Tahun,
each scored 1–3 stars by mistakes. Daily sales go into savings that buy **shop
upgrades** (bread shelf, drinks fridge, food stall), which add new products with new
prices.

The money art is stylised for learning. It is not real currency.
